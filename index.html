    Builders’ Colloquia Evenings for builders, in DC.

    Each evening takes up one big question about how people and systems work: what makes people want to do something, how far ahead anyone can really see, who gains when things stay stuck. You don’t need to know the ideas already. Bring a question, or something you’ve seen in your own work.

    No slides, and nothing said in the room is quoted by name. I’m lining up co-hosts for the first season now. Join the waitlist and I’ll write when seats open.

    I’ll only use this to invite you to sessions, and never share it.
