        The thesis Communities get stronger when people build, see, tell, and build again, together.

        Making is how people think. Someone who has built something, however small, wants to see how it fits. Seeing it, they have something worth saying. Saying it, they find the others. And the next thing they build is bigger, because they are not building it alone.

          The order Build first. The rest follows.

          I used to put seeing first. In practice it runs the other way: nobody looks hard at a system until they have put something into it. Each dot in the loop is a group of people, and each time round they come back with one more ring. With 3D on, every lap steps toward you: the loop is a spiral, and the laps are time. Click a move to switch it off and watch where people stall.

            A community is not a machine. Simple rules, followed by many people, make patterns nobody designed. So I work on the rules, not the people.
